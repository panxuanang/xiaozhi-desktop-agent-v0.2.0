# Office DOCX fallback skill

Use python-docx and deterministic formatting. For Chinese business documents prefer Microsoft YaHei/微软雅黑 where available, body around 10.5pt, first-line indent about two CJK characters, 1.5 line spacing, paragraph spacing after 6pt, no first-line indent on headings, consistent tables, and reopen the final DOCX with python-docx before delivery.
